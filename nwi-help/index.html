Already receive SNAP and lost food because of an outage, flooding, or storm damage? Submit Indiana State Form 40988 by Monday, Aug. 31. The loss must be verified.

Start with what you need

Food, water, shelter, charging, or cleanup

Call 211 or 866-211-9966, text your ZIP code to 898-211, or search online.

Search Indiana 211

Completely displaced from home

Call 211 and ask to be screened for IHCDA temporary housing assistance.

Approved households choose either a hotel stay of up to 30 days or rental help with an application fee, limited first-month rent, and a security deposit. Payments go to the hotel or landlord.

Lake or Porter County home damage

1Report the damage. This helps document the disaster but is not an aid application.

Report damage through Indiana 211

2Apply separately for state aid.

Apply for State Disaster Relief

State disaster assistance

Qualifying households may receive up to $5,000 for certain uninsured damage to a primary residence.

Eligible items can include certain home repairs, essential appliances and furnishings, debris removal, medical devices, tools, and transportation. Local-disaster-declaration and other eligibility limits apply.

Downed power line

Stay away and report it to NIPSCO at 800-464-7726. If anyone is in immediate danger, call 911.

Before applying or cleaning up

  • Take photos and video when it is safe.
  • Keep receipts for repairs, temporary housing, cleanup, and replacement items.
  • Contact your insurer promptly if you have coverage.
  • Do not pay anyone claiming to be FEMA or disaster-assessment personnel.

FEMA individual assistance is not open yet. As of this page’s verification time, Indiana’s major-disaster request was still pending. Do not submit a FEMA application unless the state announces approval.
