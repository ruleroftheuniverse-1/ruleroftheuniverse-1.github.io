Patient Advocacy and Medical Education

I help you understand medical information, prepare for care, ask informed questions, get your concerns heard, and leave with a clear account of what happens next.

Preparation and research

We work through your history, records, medications, concerns, and priorities. I organize the information into a usable account and an appointment agenda, identify unanswered questions, and examine relevant literature when it would help.

Support during care

I attend with your permission, help communicate your concerns, ask substantive questions, and take notes. I can question unclear reasoning, bring overlooked information into the discussion, and speak up when your concerns are being missed. You direct my participation.

Medical education

I explain medical terminology, research findings, and the evidence behind claims, including limitations and uncertainty. We can discuss mechanisms, published treatment options, and questions worth taking to your care team. This supports your understanding and decisions; it is not a diagnosis, prescription, or individualized treatment service.

After the visit

You receive a clear written account of the discussion, decisions, unanswered questions, and next actions. We review it together so you can understand the plan and identify what still needs attention.

About Nichole

My background includes teaching logic and biomedical ethics at UC Riverside, interpreting research, representing people within institutions, and building documentation systems. I have substantial experience navigating complex healthcare for myself and supporting others with preparation, research, and direct advocacy. I combine analytical depth with the willingness to speak up.

Settings and availability

Appointment support can include outpatient, dental, psychiatric, telehealth, emergency-department, and inpatient settings, subject to availability and facility access. My first client visits will emphasize cooperative communication. Urgent situations are not automatically excluded; this service does not replace emergency medical care or provide an on-call clinical response.

Founding client package

Discounted rate: $450 for up to four hours of preparation, record review, research, attendance, debrief, and written follow-up around one appointment. We agree the time allocation before booking. Additional work is $150 per hour with prior approval. Larger record reviews, hospital support, and travel are scoped separately. Local availability is based near Valparaiso; travel costs are agreed in advance.

Arrange support

Contact me to discuss what you need, the setting, and timing. We will agree how to transfer records and whether to use AI-assisted research or document analysis. I am an independent advocate and educator, not a licensed clinician, attorney, or board-certified patient advocate.
